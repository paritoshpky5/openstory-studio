```markdown
Hey Product Hunt! 👋

I'm thrilled to introduce **OpenStory Studio** — an open-source, local-first filmmaking suite built to solve the biggest headache in AI video: **randomness and character inconsistency.**

If you’ve ever tried making a multi-scene animated story with AI, you know the pain:
- The character's face, skin tone, and clothes change in every single clip.
- Video generation APIs burn through credits while you try to fix small camera mistakes.
- Adding native language voiceover and subtitles requires juggling 4 different video editors.

OpenStory Studio brings professional filmmaking structure to GenAI:
1. 🤖 **1-Click AI Story Planning**: Decompose raw story ideas into 3–8s shots, character bibles, and lighting prompts via OpenAI (GPT-4o), Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Google Gemini 1.5 Pro, or OpenRouter.
2. 👤 **Character & Style Bibles**: Lock down face features, skin tones, and attire tokens across every scene.
3. 🖼️ **Approved Keyframes**: Promote candidate takes to approved production frames before animating.
4. 🎥 **Image-to-Video Engine**: Animate shots with controlled camera moves via Kling AI and Seedance.
5. 🎙️ **Hindi Voice Lab**: Authentic Hindi storytelling & dialogue powered by Sarvam AI (`bulbul:v1`) and ElevenLabs, supporting traditional solo कथावाचक (Katha-Vachak) or dramatic multi-character acting.
6. 🎚️ **Deterministic FFmpeg Engine**: Assembles your timeline, auto-ducks music during dialogue, burns Devanagari captions, and exports in 1080p, 4K, or Shorts format.
7. 📦 **Complete Project Portability**: Export your entire film project into a single self-contained `.zip` file (all timeline records + all local images/audio/video) and restore it on any machine in 1 click!

✨ **The best part? It supports a 100% Free Mode ($0 Spend):**
You don't need paid API keys! You can use free daily web credits (Kling web daily 66 credits, Google ImageFX, Sarvam playground) by clicking "Copy Prompt for Web" and dropping your downloaded takes right back into the timeline.

🌟 **Current Scope & Roadmap:**
We launch today with battle-tested support for **authentic Hindi storytelling** and **Stylized 3D Animation**, and are actively expanding to regional Indian & global languages (Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, English) and new visual styles (Anime, 2D Classic, Watercolor)!

Check out the GitHub repo, run it locally with 1 click (`.\start.bat` on Windows or `./start.sh` on Mac/Linux), and let me know what you think! 🚀
```
